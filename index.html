Our Services

Powering Ballarat, Inside and Out

A full suite of electrical and solar services for homes and businesses — whether it's an urgent fix or a planned upgrade.

Emergency Electrical Repairs

Power out at 11pm? Switchboard tripping every few hours? We're on call around the clock across Ballarat to diagnose the fault and get you back up and running fast.

Solar Panel Installation & Service

Quality solar systems designed for Ballarat conditions, installed by accredited sparkies who know how to maximise your return. We also service, repair and upgrade existing systems that aren't pulling their weight.

Switchboard Upgrades & Safety

Old ceramic fuses and outdated boards are a real fire risk in older Ballarat homes. We upgrade switchboards to current Australian Standards with safety switches on every circuit.

Ceiling Fans, Power Points & Lighting

From extra power points in the home office to ceiling fans, exhaust fans and two-way switches, we handle the everyday jobs with the same care as the big ones. Clean install, no mess left behind.

Commercial Electrical Services

Fit-outs, maintenance, test and tag, and emergency repairs for shops, offices and trades across the Ballarat region. We work around your hours so your business keeps trading.

Need Something Else?

If it involves wires, switches or solar, we've probably done it. Give us a call and we'll let you know how we can help.
